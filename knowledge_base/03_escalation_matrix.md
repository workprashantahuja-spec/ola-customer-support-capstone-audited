# Escalation matrix

Immediately route P1 safety concerns to the Safety Lead, suspected account takeover to the Security Lead, and widespread outages to the Incident Lead.
Route other Billing cases to Finance Support, Account Access cases to Account Support, Technical Issue or Product Defect cases to Technical Support, and General Inquiry cases to the Support Desk.
Escalate a P2 or P3 case to the Support Lead when its acknowledgment or resolution target is missed, or when the same unresolved complaint is reported for the third time within 30 calendar days.
Finance Support approves verified billing refunds, and the Support Lead alone approves discretionary service credits within the service-credit limit.
The automated agent can explain policy, look up tickets, and recommend a handoff, but only a human can perform the handoff, change ticket status, approve compensation, or issue money or credits.
