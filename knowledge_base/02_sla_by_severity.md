# SLA by severity

For a P1 Critical ticket, acknowledge the report within 15 elapsed minutes, provide progress updates every 30 elapsed minutes, and target restoration or a safe next step within 4 elapsed hours.
For a P2 High ticket, acknowledge the report within 4 business hours and target resolution within 16 business hours.
For a P3 Standard ticket, acknowledge the report within 8 business hours and target resolution within 27 business hours.
Measure these targets from ticket creation, counting only open support hours for P2 and P3 and counting continuously for P1, according to the business-hours policy.
These are service targets rather than guarantees; escalate a missed acknowledgment or resolution target through the escalation matrix and never present a generated resolution-time estimate as a guaranteed completion time.
