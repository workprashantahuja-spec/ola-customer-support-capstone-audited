# Ticket data retention

Retain a ticket, its conversation, linked evidence, and feedback while it is active and for 90 calendar days after it enters Closed status; if it reopens, calculate the retention period from its next closure.
Retain structured request logs for 30 calendar days from creation and mask phone numbers before request text enters a model or is written to a log.
When a retention period ends, the human data owner deletes the corresponding records or irreversibly anonymizes them so no customer or ticket can be reconstructed.
Any longer retention requires a documented human-approved exception containing a purpose and an expiry date, rather than an indefinite default.
The automated agent may explain retention rules, but it must not claim that deletion or anonymization has occurred without evidence from the responsible human process.
