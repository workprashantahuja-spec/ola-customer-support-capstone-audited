# Ticket-priority classification

Classify a ticket as P1 Critical when it reports an active rider or driver safety concern, suspected account takeover, or a widespread outage that blocks rides.
Classify a ticket as P2 High for a duplicate charge, an individual account-access failure, or a service or product defect that does not meet the P1 conditions.
Classify a ticket as P3 Standard for general policy questions, feedback, or a minor issue that does not block service.
Use the highest severity supported by the reported impact; the ticket category, customer VIP status, and existing escalation flag do not determine priority on their own.
When impact is unclear, ask a clarifying question, while referring any reported P1 condition to a human immediately.
