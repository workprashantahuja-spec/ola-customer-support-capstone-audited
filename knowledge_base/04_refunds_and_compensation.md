# Refund and compensation policy

A verified duplicate charge or a charge for a cancelled or unprovided ride is eligible for a billing-refund review.
A human in Finance Support must check the linked trip and payment evidence before approving a refund, because a support-ticket status alone does not establish eligibility.
An approved billing refund returns only the verified incorrect portion of the charge to the original payment method, with a processing target of 5 business days after approval.
Goodwill compensation for a confirmed service disruption follows the separate service-credit policy and cannot reimburse a charge that has already been refunded.
The automated agent must not invent a fare, payment amount, refund approval, or refund completion date when the relevant evidence is unavailable.
