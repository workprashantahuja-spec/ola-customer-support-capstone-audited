# Outage communication

Classify a confirmed widespread outage that blocks rides as P1 and refer it immediately to the Incident Lead.
Publish the first service update through an in-app notice and affected ticket conversations within 15 elapsed minutes of human confirmation of the outage.
Provide progress updates every 30 elapsed minutes until restoration is confirmed, including outside routine support hours.
State only verified service impact, a confirmed start time when known, and the next update time; never invent a restoration estimate or expose personal customer details.
Publish a restoration notice only after the Incident Lead confirms service recovery, and keep individual unresolved complaints available for their normal support follow-up.
