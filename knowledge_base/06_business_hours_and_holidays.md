# Business hours and holiday support

Routine support operates Monday through Saturday from 09:00 to 18:00 Indian Standard Time, using the Asia/Kolkata time zone.
Routine support is closed on Sundays and on the project holidays of 26 January, 15 August, and 2 October.
P1 safety, security, and widespread-outage cases receive continuous coverage, including nights, Sundays, and holidays.
P2 and P3 business-hour clocks pause outside routine support hours and resume at the next opening; they do not reset when a new day begins.
For a business-day deadline, exclude Sundays and the listed project holidays, count the next open date as day one, and use 18:00 Indian Standard Time as the deadline on the final counted date.
