# Service-credit policy

A confirmed service failure, such as a disrupted ride caused by an outage, may qualify for a discretionary goodwill service credit after human review.
Only the Support Lead may approve a service credit, with a maximum total value of INR 200 for one incident and no automatic entitlement.
An issued service credit expires after 30 calendar days, is non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
A service credit must not reimburse a charge already covered by a billing refund, and multiple reports about the same incident do not increase the INR 200 limit.
The automated agent may explain these rules or recommend review, but it cannot issue credits or infer eligibility from the ticket status alone.
