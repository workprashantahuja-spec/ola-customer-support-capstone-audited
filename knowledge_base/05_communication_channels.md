# Customer communication channels

Use the in-app support conversation and its ticket ID as the main channel for case discussions and human handoffs.
Send an email update only to the customer's verified email address with their consent, and record the update in the same ticket conversation.
Arrange a phone callback only with customer consent and mask phone numbers in any model input or request log.
Public social-media replies may explain general policy but must not reveal ticket records, personal details, or payment information.
Support must never request an account password, a one-time password, or a full payment-card number in any channel.
