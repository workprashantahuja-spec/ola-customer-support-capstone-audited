# Feedback collection

When a ticket becomes Resolved, send one optional feedback request containing a rating from 1 to 5 and an optional comment.
A human closes the ticket when the customer confirms resolution or after 7 calendar days without a reopening request, even if the customer does not submit feedback.
A rating of 1 or 2 requires Support Lead review within 1 business day, but does not automatically change the ticket severity or approve compensation.
Invite feedback consistently from all eligible resolved cases and do not reward, suppress, or selectively request positive ratings.
Keep feedback with the associated ticket and apply the ticket data-retention policy.
